# Railway Anatomy: next learning studies

**Original proposals — 1 November 2026. Revision 8 now implements bounded teaching cases for all ten questions below. See [the release mapping and explicit limits](REVISION-8.md#applied-studies-from-the-learning-roadmap). The original proposals are retained to distinguish these cases from broader calibrated or operational capabilities.**

## Implemented baseline

The atlas already connects a whole railway to vehicles, infrastructure, stations, control and depots. Its existing studies cover AC/DC energy chains, qualitative adhesion, infrastructure work, shared dependencies, an accessible passenger route, and configuration evidence. The current communications expansion adds six equipment/connection views: locations, network hardware, SCADA, voice, passenger information and security. It includes real removable internals and bounded examples of requests, feedback, content delivery, audio output and door state. People and processes have a separate presentation.

The proposals below deepen specific unanswered questions. They do not describe those existing topics as absent. Public reading links are starting points for research; a source covering equipment roles is not sufficient evidence for a numerical simulation or a railway operating rule.

## Priorities

| Priority and proposed study | Learning question and gap beyond the baseline | Useful representation | Public reading starting point |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Trace a named supervisory point** | How do source identity, units, timestamp and quality travel with a value? The current SCADA example separates position, input, request and display; it does not model a point database or time-quality semantics. | A selectable point record beside the 3D sensor/I/O assembly and a timestamped 2D exchange. Compare a current value, delayed value and mismatched point identity using invented data. | [TfNSW electrical SCADA interface requirements](https://standards.transport.nsw.gov.au/_entity/annotation/9b648150-ce31-542c-9e35-9f042bbe5826). Use its interface distinctions; obtain explicit public evidence before adopting any point-quality convention. |
| **2. Service availability across shared dependencies** | Which services survive one lost path, and what must be checked after reconnection? The atlas currently shows selected losses and logical connections, without a network recovery model. | A small interactive topology with separate network, power, application and recording layers. Highlight the surviving endpoints and retained gaps after a chosen failure. | [NIST SP 800-82 Revision 3](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-82r3.pdf), especially availability, contingency planning and recovery. Keep the example fictional and free of operational access settings. |
| **3. Condition evidence to a maintenance decision** | What can a measurement suggest, and what further evidence distinguishes competing causes? Inspection fixtures and release boundaries exist; uncertainty and diagnosis are not yet developed into a case study. | A wheelset or component cutaway paired with repeat measurements, a confidence/quality view and a branching evidence worksheet. Retain separate diagnosis, repair and release decisions. | [ÖBB wheelset maintenance](https://ts.oebb.at/en/products-services/komponenteninstandhaltung/radsaetze). Seek additional public measurement-method research before supplying any defect or acceptance threshold. |
| **4. PA coverage and intelligibility** | Why can an energized loudspeaker still provide poor passenger information? The current assembly explains controller, amplifier and driver; it does not model room acoustics. | A 2D concourse section with speaker zones, background-noise and reverberation comparisons; link each zone to the existing amplifier/speaker cutaway. Use sourced experimental examples rather than a compliance score. | [Bosch PRAESENSA product overview (German)](https://www.boschbuildingtechnologies.com/de/de/loesungen-und-services/brandschutz/sprachalarmierung/praesensa/). Its component and zone descriptions are the starting point; acoustic calculations need additional evidence. |
| **5. Radio access along a moving railway** | How do location, access coverage and the fixed network affect an ongoing service? Cab equipment and operational voice are present, but radio coverage and handover are not simulated. | A longitudinal train/wayside section beside a service-state timeline. Compare voice and train-data requirements as distinct services; do not imply movement authority from radio availability. | [Network Rail’s GSM-R role and equipment overview](https://www.networkrail.co.uk/industry-and-commercial/gsm-r-communicating-on-the-railway/gsm-r-for-drivers-and-signallers/). Add a public radio-engineering source before drawing coverage limits or handover behaviour. |
| **6. Electrical section boundaries and local protection** | Where does one supply section end, and how is that different from a supervisory indication? AC/DC chains and component internals exist; section boundaries and protection response need their own defined scope. | A compact electrical single-line paired with the physical collector/contact system. Compare two clearly named example arrangements without creating an isolation or switching procedure. | [Siemens AC traction supply overview](https://www.mobility.siemens.com/global/en/portfolio/rail-infrastructure/electrification/ac-traction-power-supply.html). Research the selected arrangement before adding neutral sections, protection settings or transient behaviour. |
| **7. Airflow through a complete station plant path** | How do filters, coils, dampers and duct resistance change the working point of a fan? The current models expose these parts and their motion without solving airflow. | An AHU cutaway linked to a small fan/system-curve plot, using public example curves and explicit units. Keep ordinary comfort ventilation separate from any smoke-management study. | [Trane’s air-handler anatomy](https://www.trane.com/commercial/north-america/us/en/about-us/newsroom/glossary/air-handler.html) and [FläktGroup tunnel/metro ventilation overview](https://www.flaktgroup.com/en/solutions/tunnel-and-metro-ventilation/). |
| **8. Wheel–rail response with stated assumptions** | How do wheel rotation, vehicle speed, braking demand and available adhesion interact quantitatively? The existing spin/slide/modulation scenes are qualitative. | A constrained explanatory dynamics model with linked speed, slip and braking-effort plots, plus the existing wheel/rail geometry. Expose assumptions and sensitivity rather than output operational stopping distances. | [Knorr-Bremse adhesion and wheel-slide mechanisms](https://rail.knorr-bremse.com/en/us/portfolio/products-and-systems/braking-systems/adhesion/). Numerical parameters require separate public research and validation. |
| **9. Drainage, formation and structural observations** | How can water paths and support conditions affect different assets without one repair closing every observation? Track layers and a culvert/work example already exist; coupled deterioration is not modeled. | A soil/track/culvert cross-section with a time comparison and separate observation records. Show a changed drainage path while retaining an unresolved structural observation. | [Network Rail’s landslip explanation](https://www.networkrail.co.uk/rail-travel/delays-explained/landslips/). Add public geotechnical evidence before representing pore pressure, settlement or failure thresholds. |
| **10. Change impact across interfaces** | Which interface definitions and test evidence become affected when one hardware or software version changes? Existing process views distinguish installation, evidence and review; a dependency-driven impact exercise would add depth. | A 2D traceability graph linking a fictional changed item to an I/O definition, consuming application, test case and outstanding issue. Let learners identify missing evidence without granting approval. | [Crossrail’s publicly released certification-package example](https://learninglegacy.crossrail.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/CRL1-XRL-O4-GUI-CR001-50009-Rev-2.0-Certification-Package-Compilation-Process.pdf). Use the published evidence relationships, not its project forms or identifiers. |

## Selection rule for further expansion

Choose a study when it adds a learning question, a representation that can answer it, and adequate public evidence for the claimed behaviour. Reuse the existing assemblies when the new question concerns information or decisions. Use new 3D geometry when the learner needs to inspect a physical mechanism. Every calculation should expose its assumptions; every animated state should have a visible consequence; every article should explain what remains outside the example.

Primary links above were checked during this review. Supplier pages describe examples, dated guidance retains its jurisdiction and scope, and the proposed visualizations remain original educational material.
