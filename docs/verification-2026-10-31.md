# Railway Anatomy 3.0 verification

Public-source release: 163 concepts, 110 reading records, 32 model views, 26 optional tours and eight system domains.

## Executed checks

- `npm run check`: 13 content/package checks and nine geometry checks passed, plus pure technology-selection checks.
- All concept primary routes, view defaults, tour actions and variants resolve to real model anchors. Availability across selectable technology alternatives is checked separately from default geometry.
- Independent propulsion/control choices: all ten configurations normalize without coupling their axes. Actual scene checks cover LIM/rotary with loop/radio, including removing the reaction rail/primary for rotary and removing the inductive loop/pickup in radio context.
- Maintenance openings: vertical raycasts find no floor slab across inspection, bogie-drop and wheel-lathe apertures; surrounding slabs remain. The removed bogie and its drop align at the original bogie position, leaving one attached bogie.
- Source reading directory includes every reference and current component explanation; stale component text fails checks.
- Published GitHub Action version tags were resolved against the official action repositories before use.

## Browser observations

The preview browser exercised the software SVG compatibility renderer. New platform, OCC, depot, maintenance, wash and work-zone views loaded. All five control architecture choices opened their named studies while preserving the propulsion choice. Rotary plus loop remained selectable; rotary plus radio changed both relevant hardware and narrative. Component inspection and URL technology state were observed.

At a 375-pixel iframe width (360-pixel content width after its scrollbar), all eight domain buttons and both technology selectors were available with no document horizontal overflow. The model-view strip has its own horizontal navigation.

A platform-door lesson rejected a close-command-as-proof answer, accepted the independent-feedback answer, and recorded the result as completed with a retry while preserving rotary/loop choices.

## Cross-check corrections

Independent source/model review identified and closed: a slab covering maintenance openings; a duplicate/unrelated central bogie; a bent rotary transmission shaft; and prose claiming detector-health, lock-failure or water branches that were not simulated. Current text states those representation limits. Source spot checks used operator, regulator and manufacturer publications. No private railway source text, PDFs, ratings or installation drawings were added.

## Limits

Hardware WebGL rendering was not visually exercised in this environment. Geometry checks establish authored invariants and finite meshes, not real equipment dimensions or railway safety logic. Detection/braking performance, independent sensor-health and lock-failure states, maintenance procedures, energy isolation, treatment quality and commissioned technology combinations are not validated by this atlas.
